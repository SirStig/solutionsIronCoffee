I have shipped a lot of things and written about approximately none of them. That
is a waste — most of what I have learned came from someone else bothering to write
their version down.

So this is the fix. Short posts, mostly about things I actually hit:

- Reverse-engineering a 2002 game's resource format so it could be rebuilt in Swift.
- Why an all-in-one fitness platform was too much surface area for the team behind it.
- Caching that behaves the same in a unit test and in production.
- The unglamorous parts of shipping to the App Store, Play Store and the web from
  one codebase.

No tutorials I have not personally needed, and no posts written to have posted.

If something here is wrong, [tell me](mailto:joshua@ironcoffee.com). I would rather
be corrected than consistent.
